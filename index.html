    How to use this map

    One global ranking and four specialist ones, mapped across 41 countries and 15 hubs. Pick a ranking, choose how to measure it, then click any country, hub or university to open its card.

    What each control does

    • View switches between every ranked university or school plotted as a dot, and the world coloured by score.
    • Rankings moves the whole map to the Global Ranking or to one of the specialist rankings: Power, Create and Transform.
    • Next 50, set apart at the end of the first row, shows the Global Ranking's places 151 to 200. They are there to explore and are counted in no score.
    • Measure reads the map three ways: overall strength, talent density against population, or evolution 2025-2026 between the two editions.
    • Show hubs draws each city cluster as a bubble sized by the weight of the universities & schools in it, so you can see where the ranking concentrates.
    • Filter narrows to a type of school or to the top of the ranking. Every figure on screen follows.

    Reading the map

    Colour carries the score for every country. The 1 to 5 circles mark the five leaders on whichever measure is selected, so the top of the table can be read at a glance. The key on the left of the map names everything on screen.

    Finding your way

    Click a country to drill into it, then a hub inside it. Search flies straight to any country, hub or school. Reset view, at the top right of the map, returns to the whole world.

    Every control has an i beside it that explains it in place.
