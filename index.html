What are DL Points?

DL Points are a simple scoring mechanism that lets you compare one country against another, and one institution against another, on a single scale.

They are not a separate ranking or a sixth module. They are a way of turning ranking positions into a number you can add up and compare.

How the score works

Each ranking places 150 institutions. An institution’s DL Points come from where it sits on that 1–150 scale: the higher the position, the higher the score.

An institution ranked……scores
1st150 points
50th101 points
100th51 points
150th1 point

A country’s score is all of its institutions’ points added together.

And the other measures?

Talent density divides this total by a country’s population, asking who does most with what they have. Evolution compares the DL25 and DL26 editions.
